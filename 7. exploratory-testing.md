# Exploratory Testing

## Overview

Exploratory testing was performed to investigate application behavior beyond the predefined test cases.

The goal was to identify unexpected behavior, validation inconsistencies, usability issues, and undocumented application rules.

## Environment

- Application: nopCommerce Demo Store
- Browser: Google Chrome 153.0.8010.48
- OS: Windows 11 Pro 25H2
- Tester: Denys

---

## ET-001 - Checkout Address Validation

### Charter
Explore address validation during checkout with focus on country-dependent State / Province behavior.

### Areas Explored
- Required address fields
- Country selection
- State / Province options
- Countries with configured subdivisions
- Countries without configured subdivisions

### Findings
- Available State / Province values depend on the selected country.
- Countries such as the United States, Australia, Cyprus, and Kuwait provide configured subdivision options.
- For countries without configured subdivision records, the State / Province field displays "Other".
- State / Province becomes required for countries with configured subdivisions.
- The field is not visually marked as required even when a value must be selected.

### Related Bugs
- BUG-002 - State / Province field is required but is not marked as required

## ET-002 - Registration and Password Validation

### Charter
Explore the registration form with focus on password validation, required fields, and consistency of validation messages.

### Areas Explored
- Required registration fields
- Password and Confirm password combinations
- Empty password fields
- Password mismatch validation
- Password length boundaries

### Findings
- When Password is empty and Confirm password contains a value, the message "The password and confirmation password do not match." is displayed.
- When Password contains a value and Confirm password is empty, the message "Password is required." is displayed.
- Password validation messages are inconsistent and may not clearly identify the field that requires correction.

### Related Bugs
- BUG-003 - Misleading and inconsistent validation messages for Password and Confirm password fields

## ET-003 - Search and Product Discovery

### Charter
Explore product discovery with focus on consistency between product search, manufacturer filtering, product tags, product metadata, and product attribute combinations.

### Areas Explored
- Search by product name
- Search by SKU
- Advanced Search
- Manufacturer filtering
- Manufacturer metadata
- Product tags
- Product tag result pages
- Product categories
- Product attribute combinations
- Consistency between product data and different product discovery methods

### Findings

#### Finding 1 - Apple iPhone 16 is missing the Apple manufacturer assignment

Three Apple products were identified in the catalog:
- Apple MacBook Pro
- Apple iCam
- Apple iPhone 16 128GB

Searching for `Apple` returns all three products.

The Apple manufacturer page returns only:
- Apple MacBook Pro
- Apple iCam

The MacBook and Apple iCam display `Manufacturer: Apple` on their product detail pages.

The Apple iPhone 16 does not display a manufacturer and is not included in the Apple manufacturer results.

**Result:**  
The manufacturer filter behaves consistently with the configured product data, but the Apple iPhone 16 is missing the Apple manufacturer assignment.

**Related Bug:**  
- BUG-004 - Apple iPhone 16 is missing the Apple manufacturer assignment

---

#### Finding 2 - Product attribute combination SKUs are not searchable

The Nikon D5500 DSLR does not have a SKU assigned to the base product.

Its color combinations have separate SKUs:
- Black - `N5500DS_B`
- Red - `N5500DS_R`

Searching for `N5500DS_B` or `N5500DS_R` returns no products.

The same result is observed when using Advanced Search.

As a control check, searching for the regular product SKU `AP_MBP_13` successfully returns the Apple MacBook Pro.

**Result:**  
Regular product SKU search works, while SKUs assigned to Nikon product attribute combinations are not returned by the storefront search.

**Related Bug:**  
- BUG-005 - Product cannot be found by SKU assigned to a product attribute combination

---

#### Finding 3 - Product attribute combination names are not searchable

The Nikon D5500 DSLR base product can be found by searching for:

`Nikon D5500 DSLR`

However, searches using the displayed combination names return no products:

- `Nikon D5500 DSLR - Black`
- `Nikon D5500 DSLR - Red`

The Black and Red values represent product attribute combinations rather than separate base products.

**Result:**  
The storefront search appears to use the base product name and does not return the product when the displayed attribute combination name is used.

**Classification:**  
Observation / possible search limitation.

---

#### Finding 4 - Product tag assignments are inconsistent across the demo catalog

Product tag result pages were compared with the tags displayed on individual product detail pages.

The following inconsistencies were identified:

- `Nike SB Zoom Stefan Janoski "Medium Mint"` has the `jeans` tag but does not have the `shoes` tag.
- `Nokia Lumia 1020` has the `camera` tag but does not have the `cell` tag, although it belongs to the `Cell phones` category.
- `Apple iCam` has no product tags and is therefore not returned by the `camera` tag.
- `Beats Pill Wireless Speaker` has the `computers` tag.
- `Sound Forge Pro` has the `game` tag.
- `$25 Virtual Gift Card` has the `gift` tag.
- `$50 Physical Gift Card` and `$100 Physical Gift Card` have no product tags and are not returned by the `gift` tag.

The `camera` tag assigned to Nokia Lumia 1020 is considered reasonable because the product has camera-focused functionality.

Product categories and product tags are independent, so belonging to a category does not automatically require a corresponding tag.

The tag result pages consistently reflect the tags actually assigned to the products.

**Result:**  
No defect was identified in the product tag filtering functionality itself. The inconsistency is in the product tag assignments within the demo catalog.

**Related Bug:**  
- BUG-006 - Product tags are inconsistently assigned across the demo catalog

---

### Session Summary

The exploratory session identified three defects:

- BUG-004 - Apple iPhone 16 is missing the Apple manufacturer assignment.
- BUG-005 - Product cannot be found by SKU assigned to a product attribute combination.
- BUG-006 - Product tags are inconsistently assigned across the demo catalog.

Search by displayed product attribute combination name was also investigated and documented as an observation because the behavior may represent a search limitation rather than a confirmed defect.

## ET-004 - Shopping Cart

### Charter
Explore shopping cart behavior with focus on quantity boundaries, product configurations, cart persistence, and consistency of cart data.

### Areas Explored
- Quantity changes
- Quantity boundary values
- Product removal through quantity changes
- Different configurations of the same product
- Product attribute combinations
- Gift card data persistence
- Cart persistence
- Cart behavior across browser tabs
- Quantity and total recalculation

### Findings

#### Observation 1 - Cart quantity boundaries

- Setting a product quantity to `0` removes the product from the shopping cart.
- The behavior is consistent across different products.
- Negative quantities and other invalid values are not accepted.
- The maximum accepted cart quantity was verified as `10000`.
- Quantity validation and cart recalculation behaved as expected.

**Classification:**  
Expected behavior / observation.

---

#### Observation 2 - Different product configurations are handled correctly

Different configurations of `Build your own computer` were added to the shopping cart.

Each configuration was displayed as a separate cart item with the correct selected options and price.

**Classification:**  
Expected behavior.

---

#### Observation 3 - Product attribute combinations are handled correctly

Black and Red variants of `Nikon D5500 DSLR` were added to the shopping cart.

The two variants were displayed as separate cart items and retained their correct product information.

**Classification:**  
Expected behavior.

---

#### Observation 4 - Gift card data is preserved

A Virtual Gift Card was added to the shopping cart with sender and recipient information.

The entered gift card data remained available after navigating through the cart and related pages.

**Classification:**  
Expected behavior.

---

#### Observation 5 - Shopping cart state is preserved

Shopping cart contents, quantities, and related data remained consistent after page refreshes and navigation between pages.

**Classification:**  
Expected behavior.

---

#### Observation 6 - Temporary inconsistent cart state between browser tabs

During one execution, checkout displayed `Shopping cart (2)` in the page header while also showing the message:

`Your cart is empty`

The same scenario was repeated several times, but the issue could not be reproduced.

**Classification:**  
Intermittent observation / not reproducible.

---

### Session Summary

No reproducible defects were identified during this exploratory session.

Shopping cart quantity validation, product configurations, product attribute combinations, gift card data, and cart persistence behaved as expected.

One temporary inconsistent cart state was observed during multi-tab testing, but the behavior could not be reproduced.
